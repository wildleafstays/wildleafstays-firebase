# Quality and Operations Architecture

## 1. Objective

Wildleaf competes as a hospitality brand, not merely a listing directory. Therefore property readiness, service execution and issue resolution are first-class product domains.

## 2. Property onboarding standard

A property cannot become live merely because an owner completes a form. Onboarding includes structured review.

Suggested dimensions for the Wildleaf Property Standard:

- legal/ownership authorization;
- guest safety;
- fire/electrical readiness;
- hygiene and housekeeping infrastructure;
- bedroom/bathroom standards;
- linen/towel standards;
- water/hot-water reliability;
- power backup where applicable;
- access/parking/road information;
- Wi-Fi and connectivity disclosure;
- kitchen/food service readiness where applicable;
- staff readiness;
- signage/guest information;
- property photography/content quality;
- amenity verification;
- accessibility disclosures;
- maintenance condition;
- emergency contacts;
- check-in/check-out process;
- payment/deposit/cancellation policy readiness.

Checks are categorized as:

- mandatory pass;
- scored quality item;
- informational disclosure;
- improvement recommendation.

## 3. Operational dashboard

For each property, operational users should see:

- arrivals today;
- departures today;
- in-house guests;
- unassigned rooms;
- unpaid/partially paid conditions;
- special requests;
- open maintenance tasks;
- room readiness;
- unresolved guest cases;
- inventory/rate alerts.

## 4. Arrival workflow

A reservation can generate automatic tasks:

- verify guest contact;
- share arrival instructions;
- collect required ID/compliance data;
- confirm meal preference where relevant;
- assign physical unit;
- housekeeping readiness check;
- welcome setup;
- transport/parking instructions;
- balance/deposit follow-up.

## 5. Housekeeping and room status

Physical units have operational states separate from saleable inventory, for example:

- clean;
- dirty;
- inspected;
- occupied;
- out of order;
- out of service.

A unit becoming out of order can create an inventory block through the inventory domain. Housekeeping staff do not edit inventory counts directly.

## 6. Service desk

Guest and owner issues become structured cases rather than informal calls/WhatsApp only.

Case fields include:

- property/reservation;
- category;
- severity/priority;
- SLA target;
- owner/assignee;
- status;
- communication history;
- evidence/attachments;
- resolution;
- goodwill/refund linkage.

Suggested initial service targets can later be configured by brand tier and case type.

## 7. Quality audits

Quality assessments recur after onboarding. Triggers may include:

- scheduled audit;
- repeated low reviews;
- serious complaint;
- ownership/management change;
- renovation;
- safety incident.

Failed mandatory checks can automatically move the property into a restricted/suspended lifecycle requiring Wildleaf approval to return live.

## 8. Staff training records

Properties may maintain staff roster and training/compliance records for operational roles. This is particularly useful for managed properties where Wildleaf service standards are contractual.

## 9. Guest feedback loop

Reviews and support cases feed quality signals. The platform should distinguish:

- property condition issues;
- staff/service issues;
- listing accuracy issues;
- guest conduct issues;
- Wildleaf support issues.

This enables targeted remediation rather than a single star rating.

## 10. Brand standards are versioned

Quality requirements evolve. Assessment templates must be versioned so historical audits remain interpretable after standards change.
